OUR PRODUCT / FIFOO

Your day is
the game.

Fifoo is a daily planning game designed to make weight loss easier to organize—one meal, workout, rest period, and everyday task at a time.

{{PRODUCT_STATUS}}

A DAY MAP, NOT ANOTHER TO-DO LIST

See the day.
Choose the next stop.

Your personal goal is translated into a daily journey. Activities become stops, and the sequence of stops becomes a path through the day.

The daily objective is to get as close as possible to 100% of the plan’s progress target. That number describes daily activity progress—not a guarantee of weight loss or a medical outcome.

When a plan changes, Fifoo is designed to preserve completed activities and recalculate what comes next.
