Real life doesn’t stand still.
Neither should a plan.

We develop consumer software that makes everyday planning easier to navigate. Our focus is practical: help people see what comes next, make a choice, and adjust when their day changes.

Our first product is Fifoo, a mobile app that turns daily meals, workouts, rest, and tasks into a connected journey toward a personal weight-loss goal. Scheduling is at the center, with clear next actions and room for the changes that come with everyday life.
